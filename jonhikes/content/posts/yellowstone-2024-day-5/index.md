We slowly woke up as the sky brightened the small valley we were in. We had another short day ahead of us so we lay in the tent while the sun rose and waited until it had cleared the mountain.

We ate our breakfast and headed out. The campsite was nice, but we were eager to return to Heart Lake. We were going to a different campsite that was closer than the one we had left the day before.

The hike was uneventful save for moving a downed tree so we could cross a stream without getting our feet wet. There was a slight breeze and clouds in the sky which allowed us to stay relatively cool as we went. Heart Lake was soon in our view. Mount Sheridan was to the west of Heart Lake and there was a fire lookout tower at the top. We had talked about climbing up there, but 3,000 feet of elevation gain didn’t sound that fun so we decided against it.

We arrived at our campsite around 11 and got settled in before heading to the lake. We took a dip to wash the sweat of the day off and I waded in with all my clothes on to try and get some of the dried sweat and stink out of them. It seemed to have helped a little bit, but it’s hard to tell. The cold water felt nice on our sore muscles and we soaked for a while. We waded down to where a creek flowed into the lake and were greeted with even colder water.

{{ post_img(path="images/4.HEIC", alt="") }}

After our clothes had dried we headed into camp for lunch. I broke out my Mountain House Biscuits and Gravy, a Christmas gift from my wife last year. It hit the spot and was just what I needed. While Logan was away I heard some noises behind me in the bushes. As I stood up I heard heavy steps heading away from me. I grabbed my bear spray as I noticed some furry brown ears poking through the bushes. As it ran away the full body came into view and it was indeed a grizzly. It didn’t look big, but it also didn’t look like a cub. My heart was racing and I yelled a hearty “go on” to feel like I had done something besides just standing up. I’m sure the bear was attracted to the campsite due to my delicious Mountain House biscuits and gravy. The bear was thankfully gone and I hoped and prayed it would stay that way. Logan soon returned and was disappointed to have missed the encounter.

The rest of the afternoon we relaxed by sitting on the gravelly lake beach, drinking our fill of ice cold stream water, and playing some games. We played UNO for a bit, but the cards are so small it’s hard to shuffle. Also, UNO with two people can be very quick. We switched to tic-tac-toe, but didn’t last long there. I won one game, but all the rest were draws. We chatted and lounged around until dinner time. It looked like rain off and on all afternoon, but none came.

{{ post_img(path="images/5.HEIC", alt="") }}

For dinner I enjoyed some “Hey Hey Elote” mac & cheese. It was good and kind of tasted like corn so I was happy. Dinner was, of course, followed by Oreos. We headed to bed a little bit earlier than normal as we have 14 miles tomorrow and we want to be well rested. It also could rain so we want to be ready to get out of camp as quickly as we can.

Food Journal:

- Lunch: mountain house biscuits and gravy
