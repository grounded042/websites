As the sun rose we lay in our sleeping bags enjoying the warmth. We were in slow mode today since we only had 6 miles to hike to our next campsite. Despite taking our time we were still on the trail early.

Soon after getting on the trail we met two other hikers. They were on their way out, and had stayed at campsite 8H2 which is next to our campsite of 8H3 for tomorrow night. We chatted for a bit before heading on.

We soon left Heart Lake behind and hiked through a nice pine forest. We passed 4 more hikers as we went and had a sliver of hope that maybe we’d share a campsite tonight. We don’t mind being alone, but it’s always nice to chat with other hikers in camp.

We rolled into our camp, 8B2, a little after eleven and went through the usual routine. We were next to a lake so the next order of business was to check it out. Sadly it had a very muddy bottom so we didn’t get to go for a dip. We headed back to the campsite and decided to check out the nearby stream. It wasn’t much, but we made do.

We hung out at the stream soaking our feet and looking at bugs and rocks for a couple of hours. Peak entertainment in the backcountry. I took the opportunity to use my washcloth to wipe the sweat of the day off my face and arms. It wasn’t the same as a swim in a lake, but it hit the spot.

We took an afternoon snack break and explored the campsite a bit. Logan found a nice pair of antlers nearby. It would have been cool to take them home, but the park doesn’t allow that and we didn’t want to carry the extra weight.

{{ post_img(path="images/6.HEIC", alt="") }}

For dinner we had tortillas, dried pulled pork, BBQ sauce, and Chic-fil-A sauce. It was a good meal with no cleanup and we will definitely bring it on a future trip. We topped things off with Oreos and hung our food and smellables for the night.

Tomorrow would be another short day heading back to Heart Lake, and we were eager to be back to a lake we could swim in.

Food:
- Breakfast: banana bread pop-tarts, chocolate covered coffee beans. Good. Would have again as it’s nice and fast.
- Lunch: protein bars, fruit strips, cheese wheel, meat sticks, and beef jerky. For drinks I mixed dad’s root beer with strawberry crush. It was ok.
- Dinner: two tortillas, dried pulled pork, BBQ sauce, Chic-fil-A sauce. Oreos. Fantastic meal. Would do again. Try Walmart pulled pork instead as it’s cheaper. One Chic-fil-A sauce was enough. 2 oz bottle of BBQ sauce shared between us was also enough. Tortillas were amazing. Maybe next time do smaller ones.
